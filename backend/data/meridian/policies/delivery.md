# Delivery Policy

Meridian Marketplace (fictional demonstration company). Effective 2026-01-01.

## DEL-1 Same-day delivery

Same-day delivery is available for products marked same-day eligible, for
delivery addresses in Bengaluru, Mumbai or Delhi.

## DEL-2 Free delivery

Meridian Plus members get free delivery on every order whose total exceeds 500
rupees. Other customers get free delivery on orders of 999 rupees or more.
Otherwise a delivery fee of 49 rupees applies.

## DEL-3 Delayed deliveries

An order is delayed when it has not been delivered by its promised delivery date.
If a delivery is more than 3 days late, the delivery fee is refunded. Meridian
Plus members additionally receive a 100-rupee wallet credit.

## DEL-4 Order tracking

Customers can see the current status, carrier and promised delivery date of
every order from their account.
