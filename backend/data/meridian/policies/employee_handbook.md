# Employee Handbook: Leave, Travel and Expenses

Meridian Marketplace (fictional demonstration company). Effective 2026-01-01.

## LV-1 Annual leave

Employees receive 18 days of annual leave per year. Employees with at least 3
completed years of service receive 24 days.

## LV-2 Sick leave

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year.

## LV-3 Parental leave

Employees receive 26 weeks of paid parental leave.

## TRV-1 Business travel reimbursement caps

Business travel expenses are reimbursed up to a per-trip cap that depends on the
employee's grade: 5,000 rupees for grade L1, 8,000 rupees for grade L2, and
15,000 rupees for grade L3. The reimbursement is the amount claimed or the cap,
whichever is lower.

## TRV-2 Minimum distance

A trip qualifies for travel reimbursement only if the distance travelled is at
least 50 km.

## TRV-3 Own-vehicle mileage

When an employee travels in their own vehicle, the reimbursement is further
limited to a mileage allowance of 9 rupees per km.

## TRV-4 Receipts

Receipts are required for any travel claim above 2,000 rupees.

## TRV-5 Submission deadline

Travel claims must be submitted within 30 days of the trip.
