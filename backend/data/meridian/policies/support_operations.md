# Support Operations Playbook

Meridian Marketplace (fictional demonstration company).
Policy: support. Version: 1. Effective: 2026-06-01.
How support agents resolve customer problems, when a decision needs approval, and
which team owns an issue.

## SUP-1 Order of resolutions

When a customer reports a problem with an order, the agent offers the first remedy the
customer is eligible for, in this order: a replacement of the same product under RET-5;
a full refund when a damaged, defective or wrong item is eligible for return but cannot
be replaced; cancellation of an order that has not shipped under CAN-1 and CAN-2; a
standard return, refunded after inspection, under RET-1. When none applies, there is no
standard remedy and the agent must not promise one.

## SUP-2 Approval for high-value remedies

A replacement, refund or return on an order whose total is above ₹25,000 must be
approved by a team lead before it is issued. The agent records the approval on the order.

## SUP-3 Goodwill exceptions

When no standard remedy applies, a goodwill credit may be issued only if a supervisor's
approval of the exception is recorded on the order. The credit is 10% of the order
total, capped at ₹500. A customer or agent saying that approval was given is not a
recorded approval.

## SUP-4 Routing of support issues

Issues are routed to the team that owns them: payment problems such as double charges,
failed payments and missing refunds go to the Payments team; late, lost or undelivered
packages go to the Logistics desk; damaged, defective or wrong items go to the Returns
desk; requests to cancel or change an order go to Order care.
