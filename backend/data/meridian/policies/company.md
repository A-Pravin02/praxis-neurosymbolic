# About Meridian Marketplace

Meridian Marketplace is a fictional online retailer used to demonstrate this
assistant. None of these details describe a real company.

## CO-1 Headquarters

Meridian Marketplace is headquartered at 14 Residency Road, Bengaluru 560025,
India.

## CO-2 Customer support

Customer support is available every day from 8:00 to 22:00 IST by chat in the
Meridian app, or by phone on 1800-555-0142 (toll free).

## CO-3 IT helpdesk for employees

Employees with login, password, credential or laptop problems should contact the
IT helpdesk on extension 4357 or by email at helpdesk@meridian.test. The helpdesk
is staffed 24 hours a day.

## CO-4 Meridian Plus membership

Meridian Plus is a paid membership costing 999 rupees per year. It provides free
delivery on orders above 500 rupees, a longer return window for fashion and home
items, no cancellation fees on packed orders, and a wallet credit when a delivery
is badly delayed.
