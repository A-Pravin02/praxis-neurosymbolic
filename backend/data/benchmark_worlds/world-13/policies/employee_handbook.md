# Employee Handbook: Leave, Travel and Expenses

Meridian Marketplace (generated evaluation company).

## LV-1 Annual leave

Employees receive 15 days of annual leave per year. Employees with at least
2 completed years of service receive 18 days.

## TRV-1 Business travel reimbursement caps

Business travel expenses are reimbursed up to a per-trip cap that depends on the
employee's grade: 4,000 rupees for grade L1, 6,500 rupees for grade L2, and
13,500 rupees for grade L3. The reimbursement is the amount claimed or the cap,
whichever is lower.

## TRV-2 Minimum distance

A trip qualifies for travel reimbursement only if the distance travelled is at
least 40 km.

## TRV-3 Own-vehicle mileage

When an employee travels in their own vehicle, the reimbursement is further
limited to a mileage allowance of 12 rupees per km.

## TRV-4 Receipts

Receipts are required for any travel claim above 2,500 rupees.

## TRV-5 Submission deadline

Travel claims must be submitted within 21 days of the trip.
