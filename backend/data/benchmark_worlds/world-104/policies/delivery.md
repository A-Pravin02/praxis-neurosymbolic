# Delivery Policy

Meridian Marketplace (generated evaluation company).

## DEL-1 Same-day delivery

Same-day delivery is available for products marked same-day eligible.

## DEL-2 Free delivery

Plus members get free delivery on every order whose total exceeds 599
rupees. Other customers get free delivery on orders of 1,199 rupees or more.
Otherwise a delivery fee of 69 rupees applies.

## DEL-3 Delayed deliveries

An order is delayed when it has not been delivered by its promised delivery date.
If a delivery is more than 2 days late, the delivery fee is refunded. Plus
members additionally receive a 75-rupee wallet credit.
